This is the first entry in a small writing space I'm carving out alongside the
work. The plan is to keep notes on the things I build on the web: the
decisions, the trade-offs, and the details that are easy to overlook.

## Why write here

Most of what I do lives in projects and client work, where the reasoning behind
a choice rarely makes it past the commit message. Writing it down forces me to
slow down and explain it properly, and that usually makes the thinking better.

## What to expect

- Short, focused pieces over long ones
- A bias toward fundamentals that age well
